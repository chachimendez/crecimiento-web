## What is the Horizen Acceleration Season?

A structured, hands-on program designed to take your startup from a first Vela integration to a working product with a clear go-to-market strategy, in six weeks.

It's a builder program where you ship a real product on Vela, get direct support from the Horizen team, and pitch on Demo Day in front of investors and ecosystem leaders.

The program follows a three-phase arc: Build, Grow, Show, opening with an onboarding week.

- **Onboarding (Week 1):** Onboard to the Vela landscape, meet the Horizen team, and lock your integration plan. You'll have direct access to Horizen's technical team through office hours from day one.
- **Build (Weeks 2–3):** Heads-down building with weekly office hours and technical pairing. Ship a working prototype: a real WASM job inside Vela with a verifiable result settling onchain.
- **Grow (Weeks 4–5):** Stress-test your business model, nail your GTM strategy, and figure out how you'll make money through your product. Mentors challenge your assumptions and the Horizen BD team opens doors.
- **Show (Week 6):** Refine your pitch, polish your demo, and deliver at the online Demo Day. This is where you prove what you've built, and what comes next.

## What is Vela?

Vela is a confidential coprocessor: you package your logic as a WASM job, run it inside a sealed TEE (trusted execution environment), and settle the result onchain. In Vela's own words: "Run logic in private. Settle publicly onchain".

It's built for the cases where sensitive data and public rails need to coexist: from tokenized asset issuers seeking liquidity on public chains to trading firms protecting proprietary strategies. Local development runs in Docker with an emulated TEE, and the live environment is Base Sepolia, with Base mainnet next.

Vela is in early access, which means the teams in this cohort build alongside the people making it, and shape the product as they go.
